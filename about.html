Established in December 2024, Klasse Production Sdn Bhd is a Kuala Lumpur-based creative and production house. Our registered business scope spans three connected disciplines:

Events

Organisation, promotion, and management of events — from concept through on-ground delivery.

Advertising

Creative direction, campaigns, and paid media that connect brands with the right audiences.

Film & Video Production

Motion picture, video, and television programme production — built with cinematic precision.
